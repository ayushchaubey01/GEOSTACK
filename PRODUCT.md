# GEOSTACK — 3D ULPIN Bengaluru Prototype (SIH26011)

## Product Overview & Vision
GEOSTACK is a prototype 3D geospatial cadastre and property intelligence visualization platform for urban land administration in Bengaluru. It enables municipal land-record officers and evaluators to trace land parcels from a macro 2D geographical view down to 3D volumetric building models and estimated vertical floor/unit layouts.

## Target Users
1. **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Evaluators & Jury**: Assessing technical innovation, architectural rigor, UI clarity, and adherence to cadastre standardization.
2. **Municipal Land-Record & Cadastral Officers**: Verifying parcel boundaries, spatial property records, and vertical land parcel identification.
3. **Urban Planners & Revenue Administrators**: Inspecting volumetric density, floor distributions, and spatial property registries.

## Core Mission
- Trace from **2D Land Parcel** $\to$ **3D Building Volumetric Model** $\to$ **Estimated Floor & Unit Cadastre**.
- Provide transparent attribution, source tracking, and confidence scoring at every hierarchical level.

## Product Tone & Aesthetic
- Clean, authoritative, light-theme architectural tool (Linear/Whimsical aesthetic).
- Focus on clarity, precise typography (Inter + JetBrains Mono), crisp technical hierarchy, and zero clutter.

## Strict Honesty & Integrity Rules
The following disclaimers and labels are mandatory and must be strictly maintained across all user interfaces:
1. **Synthetic Demo Data**: Floor divisions, unit boundaries, room partitions, flat owner names, and demo parking slots/vehicles are synthetic demo estimates. Real interior floor plans and owner registries are not collected.
2. **Internal Prototype ID**: Prototype IDs are generated for demonstration purposes and are never represented as official government-issued ULPINs.
3. **Estimated Volumetrics**: Building heights, floor heights, and areas are derived estimates (LoD1/LoD2 volumetric models).
4. **Planned Integrations**: Features such as live RERA integration, municipal registry sync, and deed verification are designated as "Planned / Phase 2".
